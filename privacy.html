Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Gmail Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. No other users' data is accessed.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission (Google scope gmail.modify), the app reads inbox email messages and attachments, applies labels, and moves messages to Trash or Spam.

How data is used

  • Email content is sent to TypeSafe AI (Jev) to sort it into categories, and to Anthropic (Claude) to sort uncertain emails and summarize bills and emails that need a reply.
  • Summaries are delivered only to the owner's private Slack workspace.
  • Email content is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

What is stored

Email bodies and attachments are not stored. The app stores only sender addresses with the owner's keep or delete decisions, the message IDs of emails waiting for a decision, and aggregate counts. These are kept in the owner's own AWS account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
